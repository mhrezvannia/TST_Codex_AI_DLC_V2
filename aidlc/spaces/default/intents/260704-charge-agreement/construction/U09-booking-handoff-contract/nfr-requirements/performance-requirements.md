# Performance Requirements - U09

## Source Alignment

Consumes `business-logic-model.md`, `business-rules.md`, `requirements.md`, and `technology-stack.md`.

## Targets

Active lookup contract remains under 300 ms locally for normal data. Contract examples should be executable or easy to reproduce.

## Validation

Contract tests or smoke examples prove match, no-match, inactive exclusion, and invalid query behavior.
