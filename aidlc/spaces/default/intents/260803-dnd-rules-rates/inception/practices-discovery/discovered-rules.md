# Discovered Rules - W3-01 D&D Rules & Rates

**Evidence base:** [code-structure.md](../../../codekb/TST_Codex_W3-01/code-structure.md), [technology-stack.md](../../../codekb/TST_Codex_W3-01/technology-stack.md), [dependencies.md](../../../codekb/TST_Codex_W3-01/dependencies.md), [code-quality-assessment.md](../../../codekb/TST_Codex_W3-01/code-quality-assessment.md), [architecture.md](../../../codekb/TST_Codex_W3-01/architecture.md), and [business-overview.md](../../../codekb/TST_Codex_W3-01/business-overview.md)

## Mandated

- ALWAYS preserve W2-03 Rate/RateVersion, Agreement/AgreementVersion, `pricing.v1`, and signed-fixture compatibility through additive evolution.
- ALWAYS keep D&D commercial authority inside Charge and use bounded provider contracts for Booking consumption.
- ALWAYS deliver W3-01 as a risk-first vertical with domain, persistence, API, UI, and evidence in the slice that proves the outcome.
- ALWAYS require at least 80% changed-line coverage for touched Charge backend and Charge UI production code.
- ALWAYS require deterministic D&D boundary tests, dual-signed fixtures, Playwright accessibility, isolated Compose, demo guards, `aidlc-audit`, and `erp-fidelity-audit` before release claims.
- ALWAYS keep required security checks fail-closed and disclose unavailable scanners or unproven controls.

## Forbidden

- NEVER create a new D&D service, move pricing authority into Booking or Container Movement, or share service persistence.
- NEVER claim DCSA certification, invoice-level legal compliance, production deployment readiness, or production availability from local/static evidence.
- NEVER waive a required security gate or report green security evidence when pinned scanners did not execute.
- NEVER introduce module-local shell, palette, typography, component library, or marketing composition in the Charge UI.
- NEVER edit approved historical pricing versions in place or silently fall back to UTC for port-local calculation.
