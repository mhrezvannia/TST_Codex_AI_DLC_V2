## Research Questions Resolved from Evidence

- **Which comparable solutions exist?** SAP Transportation Management, Oracle Transportation Management, and CargoWise provide relevant agreement/rate, shipment, visibility, and logistics-operation benchmarks. Current LinerCore workbenches are the migration comparator.
- **What do customers expect as table-stakes?** Filterable operational lists, exact record navigation, related-domain context, lifecycle/status visibility, accessible interaction feedback, and recoverable error states.
- **What is differentiating for W4-01?** A smaller, domain-owned surface that exposes the existing provider truth consistently in one shell, rather than a broad configurable suite.
- **What is the commercial model?** External suites carry subscription/module, implementation, integration, data-migration, training, and switching costs. The selected build path carries focused engineering and ongoing shared-platform maintenance cost while preserving sunk investment in the closed services.
- **What is the audience size?** The initiative serves a bounded internal operational population. Seat counts are not required to decide the UI composition path and should be measured from identity/usage telemetry if later needed.
